We build scripts, models and tools for FiveM roleplay servers, from small free utilities to full systems. Everything here is **free**, under the [Pigeon Studios Free Licence](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/.github/blob/main/licences/free/LICENSE.md). Our paid resources are on [our store](https://pigeon-studios.tebex.store), and are added to your Cfx.re account the moment you buy.

### Free releases

| | |
|---|---|
| [**Pigeon Dice**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-dice) | Roleplay dice for FiveM: anything from a d20 to `2d6+3`, advantage, Fate dice, coins and ranges, shown in chat and above your head |
| [**Pigeon Property Watch**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-property-watch) | Finds properties nobody has used for weeks and reports them to Discord, for NoLag Properties and Qbox properties |
| [**Pigeon Vehicle Access**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-vehicle-access) | Decide who can drive which vehicles, by job, grade, gang, ACE permission or whitelist, for Qbox and QBCore |
| [**Pigeon Bucket Monitor**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-bucket-monitor) | Logs routing bucket changes to Discord, warns when players are stuck in the wrong bucket, and gives staff commands to check and fix them |
| [**Pigeon No NPC Aircraft**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-npc-aircraft) | Stops NPC planes and helicopters from spawning or flying over your map, without touching players' aircraft |
| [**Pigeon No Blind Fire**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-blind-fire) | Stops players shooting blind from cover: they have to aim out before they can fire |
| [**Pigeon No Auto Helmet**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-auto-helmet) | Stops GTA putting a helmet on players automatically when they get on a bike or into an aircraft |
| [**Pigeon No Helmet Armour**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-helmet-armour) | Removes the hidden headshot protection GTA gives helmets, so a helmet is only a look |
| [**Pigeon No Weapons In Vehicles**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-weapons-in-vehicles) | Keeps weapons holstered in the vehicles you choose, such as police helicopters or buses |
| [**Pigeon Enhanced Check**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-enhanced-check) | Scans your server's resources and settings and lists what to change before moving to FiveM for GTAV Enhanced |
| [**Pigeon Driveby Rules**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-driveby-rules) | Sets who can shoot from vehicles, from which seats, up to what speed, with which weapons and in which vehicles |
| [**Pigeon Engine**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-engine) | Start and stop engines with a key, keep them running when you get out, and leave the wheels turned |
| [**Pigeon Lean Out**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-lean-out) | Lets passengers lean out of the window and shoot all the way round, with any weapon you allow |
| [**Pigeon Notes**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-notes) | Write notes and leave them on walls, tables and floors for other players to find and read |
| [**Pigeon Seatbelt**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-seatbelt) | Buckle up with a key, or go through the windscreen in a crash, with a warning light and a chime |
| [**Pigeon Keep Hats**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-keep-hats) | Stops hats, helmets and glasses getting knocked off when a player is punched or pushed |
| [**Pigeon No Combat Roll**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-combat-roll) | Stops players combat rolling while they aim a gun, or limits them to one roll every few seconds |
| [**Pigeon No Health Regen**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-health-regen) | Stops GTA healing hurt players by itself, or slows it down and sets how far it heals |
| [**Pigeon No Idle Cam**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-idle-cam) | Stops GTA's cinematic idle camera from taking over when a player stands or sits still |
| [**Pigeon No NPC Loot**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-npc-loot) | Stops NPCs dropping their weapons when they die, and stops police cars and other vehicles handing out free guns |
| [**Pigeon No One-Shot Headshots**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-one-shot-headshots) | Stops headshots killing players in one shot, so a shot to the head does the same damage as one to the body |
| [**Pigeon No Pistol Whip**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-pistol-whip) | Stops players hitting people with the gun in their hand, by blocking melee attacks while they hold one |
| [**Pigeon No Seat Shuffle**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-no-seat-shuffle) | Stops passengers sliding into the driver's seat by themselves, with a key and /seat to change seats on purpose |
| [**Monday to Discord**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/monday-to-discord) | Posts monday.com board activity to Discord, routed by rules |
| [**Pigeon Versions**](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/pigeon-versions) | The version registry and update checker every Pigeon Studios resource uses |

Download from each repository's **Releases** page, not the green Code button. Releases are the tested, ready-to-install builds.

### Getting help

Read the resource's README first; most questions are answered in its Troubleshooting section. Then ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/pigeon-studios-uk/discussions/categories/q-a) or the support channels of our [Discord](https://discord.gg/2NFVWEUmDF). See [Support](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) for what to include.

- **Found a bug?** Open an issue in that resource's repository.
- **Have an idea?** Post it in [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/pigeon-studios-uk/discussions/categories/ideas), or vote for one that's already there.
- **News and releases** are posted in [Announcements](https://github.com/orgs/pigeon-studios-uk/discussions/categories/announcements).
- **Found an exploit or vulnerability?** Don't post it publicly. Follow our [Security Policy](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **Want to contribute?** Read [Contributing](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/pigeon-studios-uk/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
